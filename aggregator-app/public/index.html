Aggregator Platform — pools small sellers into one block

connecting…
Your role: the aggregator — a middleman between many small sellers and one bulk buyer.
Your job: discover small sellers' offers, pool several into one bigger block, publish that block (from then on it behaves exactly like a normal seller offer to the rest of the network), and once it's bought and settled, split the real proceeds back to the small sellers you pooled.
Who talks to whom: small sellers only ever interact with seller-app's own dashboard (they publish once, then wait — they never see you or the bulk buyer directly). The bulk buyer only ever interacts with buyer-app's dashboard, discovering and buying your published block like any other offer — they never see the individual pooled sellers.
You are the only one who sees both sides and does the pooling/splitting math.

1 · Discover real offers to pool

Asks buyer-app to run a real discover, then lists what it actually found on the shared network. Not a simulation.

2 · Bulk-buyer requirements (from Grid/DISCOM)

Demand-first matching: a requirement is posted by the Grid/DISCOM dashboard (quantity + a price ceiling). "Recommend" runs a cheapest-first match against currently discovered offers — it never proposes anything above the ceiling, and clearly flags a shortfall instead of hiding one.
